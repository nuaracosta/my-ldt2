Why LDT?

As much as I love teaching, I also love the back-end side of it&the side where the teacher is allowed to be creative, explore and iterate learning experiences. With this in mind, I was looking for a master's program where I would be able to explore the behind-the-scenes of teaching, while also allowing space for technology and creativity. This is how Learning, Design and Technology became an option, and ultimately, the goal.

What are you curious about?:

Education

I'm interested in seeing how education will change in the age of AI and accessible information to all. I also find it important to see how equity in the classroom will be affected by such technology.

STEM teaching

As a former Computer Science student, I became incredibly interested in STEM teaching in general, in observing how the delivery differs from Humanities classes and how the pedagogy usually lacks compared to humanities. I'm passionate about bridging the gap between STEM and underrepresented groups in the classroom, as a way to democratize access to this field.

Learning

Learning has been a recent passion of mine. I've always been the kid to enjoy learning, but over the course of my life, I lost the spark&mostly due to how the educational system does not encourage experimentation and failure. However, in the recent years I rediscovered my love for learning and the different techniques that can be applied to effectively learn a subject.
